Today: [What are LLMs?](https://huggingface.co/learn/agents-course/en/unit1/what-are-llms) from the Hugging Face Agents Course.

The part that stuck most was **tokens** — LLMs don't read text as words or characters, they read it as tokens, chunks of text (often sub-words) mapped to numbers via a tokenizer. Everything downstream — context length, cost, even weird model behavior on rare words — traces back to this.

This actually pulled up an old memory: a tokenization module from an NLP class back in my master's, well before LLMs were the default lens for "NLP." Same core idea, different era. Nice to see the thread connect — it means I already had the vocabulary, just not the framing for how central tokens are to how modern LLMs actually operate.
